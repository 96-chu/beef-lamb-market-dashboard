# Report Design Notes

The Power BI report is designed around three business questions:

1. How are beef and lamb exports and production moving?
2. Which destinations are driving growth or decline?
3. What planning range should the business use for the next forecast year?

Recommended visual structure:

- KPI cards across the top.
- Product slicer and scenario slicer.
- Quarterly trend line or combo chart.
- Destination ranking bar chart.
- Forecast scenario band chart.
- Business signals table for narrative explanation.
